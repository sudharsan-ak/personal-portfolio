# Project: Haven TV Launcher

## Overview
A remote-first, ad-free, customizable home screen launcher for Android TV and Google TV that replaces sponsored home rows with organized app collections, cinematic themes, rotating photo wallpapers, Watch Now, live NBA information, and advanced TV controls.

## Technologies
Kotlin, Java, Jetpack Compose, Android TV, ADB

## Key Features & Achievements
- Kingdom and Tide themes with day/night styling and rotating photo wallpapers
- Custom app collections, universal search, Watch Now, and live NBA views
- Integrated ADB setup, Private DNS controls, and reversible system cleanup
